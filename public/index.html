About Quiz Race

Quiz Race is a working browser-based multiplayer quiz prototype made by the AI Club.

It is designed for college events, competitions and fun activities. Players join with a room code, race through timed questions, earn points based on speed and accuracy, and finish with a live leaderboard.

No app installation is required. Open the game in a browser and join the room created by the host.
